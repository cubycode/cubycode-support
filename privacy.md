# Privacy Policy for PikLab

**Effective date:** November 17, 2026  
**Last updated:** November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PikLab ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App on iPhone or iPad. PikLab is developed and distributed by Umar Muzaffar (Cubycode).

PikLab is a photo studio. You can take or choose a photo, apply filters and edits on the device, and ask for an AI look such as a hairstyle, hair colour, beard, makeup, outfit, jewellery, tattoo, or age change. There is no PikLab account. We do not sell your information.

Contact: [cubycode@gmail.com](mailto:cubycode@gmail.com)

## 1. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following on the device:

- Photos you save in PikLab, including a smaller preview, and a week-by-week Memories video built from those photos.
- Looks you generate during a session, so you can switch back to one you already made.
- A style profile from the on-device Style Advisor: an estimated face shape, a colour season, and the suggestions that follow from them.
- A random device ID, created once and kept in the App’s settings on this device. It is not your name and it is not your Apple ID.
- Whether you allowed AI looks, and whether you confirmed that you are 18 or older for body edits.
- How many free AI looks you have used, how many paid looks remain, and the Apple transaction IDs already applied on this device. The balance is kept in the iOS Keychain and in the App’s local storage.
- Choices such as the audience you picked and whether you have seen the intro.

Deleting PikLab removes this data from the device. A photo you saved to the Photos app stays in your photo library.

## 2. What stays on the device

Filters, adjustments, lenses, stickers, text, cutouts, spot healing, collages, the duo split view, and the Style Advisor run on the device. The Style Advisor uses Apple’s Vision face landmarks and the colours in the photo. Those steps do not upload the photo.

The camera is used when you take a picture. The photo library is used when you pick a picture, and again only if you save a picture to Photos. PikLab does not scan your photo library in the background. PikLab does not ask for your location, contacts, or microphone.

## 3. AI looks

Before the first AI look, the App asks permission to send the photo. If you allow it, the photo is prepared as a compressed JPEG and sent to Google Gemini so the model can return the edited picture. A clothing photo you pick for a try-on is sent the same way, as a second image. The request also includes the instruction for that look and the random device ID, which is used to apply usage limits.

PikLab does not keep a copy of the photo it sends, and it does not use that photo to build an advertising profile. Google processes the image to produce the result. Google’s privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Gemini API terms: [https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).

The result stays on your device. You can save it in PikLab, save it to Photos, or share it. Sharing sends the picture only to the app you choose, such as Messages, Mail, Instagram, or WhatsApp.

## 4. Purchases

AI looks are the only paid feature. The first two successful AI photos are free. After that, each successful AI photo uses one look from a pack you buy. A look that fails does not use a credit. Filters, stickers, text, and saving stay free.

Payments go through the Apple App Store. Apple processes the charge. PikLab does not receive your card number. The App learns which pack you bought and adds those looks on this device.

Apple’s privacy policy: [https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)

## 5. Analytics and crash reports

PikLab uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to learn which screens are opened and which features are used, and to diagnose crashes. Events can include the name of a filter or look, the style category, whether an AI look succeeded, how long it took, and the pack you bought. A description you type can contribute a short label. These events do not include your photo.

Firebase receives the random device ID as an analytics user ID, and Crashlytics can attach that same ID to a crash report. Firebase also receives its usual technical data, such as an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, and coarse information Google derives from the network. We do not use Firebase for advertising, and we do not sell your information.

Google’s privacy policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Firebase privacy information: [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 6. Children

PikLab is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Outfits, jewellery, tattoos, finishing touches, and age changes ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older and that the photo is of you. Those edits are not for photos of children. If you believe a child under 13 has used PikLab, email [cubycode@gmail.com](mailto:cubycode@gmail.com).

## 7. Your choices

You can refuse the AI permission and keep using the on-device tools. You can turn off camera or photo access in iOS Settings. You can delete photos you saved inside PikLab by deleting the App. Because there is no account, we cannot delete a photo that is only on your device, and we cannot pull back a picture you already shared.
