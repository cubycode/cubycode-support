# Support for PikLab

PikLab is a photo studio for iPhone and iPad. It is developed and distributed by Umar Muzaffar (Cubycode).

Email [cubycode@gmail.com](mailto:cubycode@gmail.com) and include your iPhone or iPad model, the iOS version, and what you expected to happen. This address is for app help, privacy questions, purchase questions, and reports of a result that should not have been made.

## What we can see

There is no PikLab account and no library of your photos on our side. A picture in the App cannot be looked up from an email, and a photo that exists only on your device cannot be restored from here. Unused AI looks are stored on the device that bought them.

## The camera or photo library will not open

In iOS Settings, open PikLab and allow Camera to take a picture, and Photos to pick or save a picture. PikLab does not need Location, Contacts, or Microphone.

## An AI look failed or looks wrong

Use a clear, front-facing photo of yourself, or of an adult who agreed. Hair, colour, beard, and makeup stay closer to the original face. Outfits, jewellery, tattoos, finishing touches, and age changes ask you to confirm that you are 18 or older and that the photo is of you. Those edits are refused for a photo of a child.

A look that does not return a picture does not spend a credit. If the App says you have used today’s free looks, or that the service is busy, wait and try again. The first two successful AI photos are free. After that, each successful AI photo uses one purchased look. Filters, stickers, text, and saving stay free without a pack.

## Purchases

Packs are 5, 15, or 40 AI looks. The price is the price shown in the App before you confirm. Apple charges it to your Apple ID.

Looks are added on the device that completed the purchase. They are not an account balance. Installing PikLab on another device, or deleting and reinstalling it, does not bring unused looks back. For a refund, use [Apple’s report a problem page](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Apple handles the payment and the refund.

## Photos you saved

Pictures you save inside PikLab stay in the App, grouped by week in Memories, and also appear in the App’s camera roll. A week video is made on the device from those photos. Saving to the Photos app is separate: that copy stays in Photos after you delete PikLab. Sharing sends only the picture you pick, to the app you pick.

## Report a result

Email [cubycode@gmail.com](mailto:cubycode@gmail.com) if a result depicts a child, depicts someone without their agreement, or is otherwise something the App should have refused. Describe the look you requested. Do not send a photo of a child.
